Neq'at Student Led Humanitarian Organization

Context: Co-founding Neq'at

The establishment of Neq'at, in my opinion, represents one of my major accomplishments. Many of the activities and community service projects that I have mentioned above would not have taken place had Neq'at not existed. Thanks to Neq'at, I have managed to build partnerships, organize volunteers, arrange events and collect assistance for needy individuals and communities. Being one of the founders of the organization, I have gone through a lot of obstacles during the formation process of Neq'at. At first, it was quite difficult for us to recruit the necessary number of members who would be ready to dedicate themselves to the project and would manage to gain some level of reputation among other community service organizations and potential donors. Planning various events and arranging volunteer work also posed some difficulties. However, being a member of Neq'at has helped me develop many important skills such as leadership, communication and coordination. Furthermore, I am sure that Neq'at has made me believe even more strongly that any group of people is capable of making a difference if they have a common goal.

Closing remarks

I am grateful to have had the opportunity to contribute to my community and fulfill my civic responsibilities, even in a small way and at an early stage. I am proud to have been part of efforts that supported citizens in need, particularly those affected by conflict. Our initiatives have provided assistance not only to individuals in Addis Ababa, but also to communities in other regions impacted by drought and conflict, among other challenges.
